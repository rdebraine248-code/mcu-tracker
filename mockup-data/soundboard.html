S.H.I.E.L.D. datapad · audio proposal 3

Datapad Sound Board

Every sound the datapad would make, in one place, before any of it goes into the app. Tap to hear each one. Each row says which animation it belongs to. The same sounds are wired into the test build, so you can hear each one with its animation; this page is for hearing them on their own. Tell me by name which work and which don't.

One language: a S.H.I.E.L.D. terminal. Short, clean digital tones, quantized pitch steps instead of smooth slides, tight data chatter, scanning sweeps, servos and seals for the hardware. No air, no music, no reverb washes.
Quiet by default: taps sit far under everything else; only events and sequences reach full level.
Every animation covered: the first boot, every page and path change, the timeline drawing, the map, Comms and the indexes, not just the eggs. The eggs come after this set is right.
