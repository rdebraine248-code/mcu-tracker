# Audit part 1, extra pass: people with no group

Each record in your chunk is a person in a Marvel watch-tracker whose `groups` list is EMPTY. A random recheck found some of these
people do belong to a group the whole time they're on screen (Domingo Colon runs the Colon crime family; Man-Bull, Porcupine and
Saracen are in the Abomaste). Look ONLY for that: a group the person is in DURING a title they're on file for.

## Fields
`groups` = `[group, title]` stages ("(from the start)" when they hold one group from their first title). `steps` = `[title, what they
do, status]` for every title they're on file for ("In episode N," = episode N of the step's title). `twin` = their other file.

## Rules (the owner's)
- Count it only if the person is a member (or leader) during a title they're on file for. A group they left before their first title
  is backstory: no. Being captured, held, hypnotized or mind-controlled: not membership. Acting alone with hired help: no.
- Workplaces, schools and bands: do NOT propose, the owner is deciding those separately. Organisations, teams, gangs, crime families,
  armies, agencies, cults, governments, tribes and nations count.
- Use a group name from /tmp/claude-0/-home-user-mcu-tracker/74605646-3f88-5dd6-bcf2-d1299d106f7b/scratchpad/aud/people/group_names.json
  when one fits (exact spelling). A new name only if the source names the group plainly; say so in the evidence.
- If they also leave it during their titles, add the departure: ["Independent", title]. Titles must be ones the record's steps use.
- An unsure case keeps what's there (no change).

## Method (required)
Fetch every person's wiki page (MCU wiki for the main MCU, Marvel Database / the right fandom wiki for others) and read the biography
and affiliations against `steps`. Do not answer from memory.
- Wikipedia raw: `https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=PAGE&action=raw`
- Fandom API: `https://<wiki>.fandom.com/api.php?action=parse&page=PAGE&prop=wikitext&format=json` (marvelcinematicuniverse, marvel,
  xmenmovies, spiderman, marvelanimated). curl with `User-Agent: mcu-tracker-research/1.0`. Never send personal details.
Keep downloads in the folder named in your task.
