# This run: film people the screen never names (the owner's ruling, 10 November 2026)

Everyone in batches 91 to 97 was left out before only because the film never says or shows their name. The owner has ruled:
"Their names needed to be said on screen is a bad rule i think. May times maybe characters won't have their names mentioned." So
for these people, and ONLY in films:
- The name test is gone. Their file's `n` is their CREDITED name, as the batch gives it (cleaned of wiki disambiguation such as
  "(Earth-10005)" or "(Doctor)": "Richard Nixon", "Dr. Graham"). Check the credits spelling against the film's cast list on
  Wikipedia when in doubt (e.g. "Bill Drake" or "William Drake"). Where a person is credited by a codename that the film does use
  for them (Quill is credited "Kid Omega"), use the name the film's credits give and put the other as an alias.
- No staging of the name (it is never said, so there is no later point it becomes known): one name from their first title.
- Still binding: LINES and a PART IN THE PLOT. Drop anyone who turns out to have no lines or no part, as before.
- Spoiler rule still applies to everything else: a step says only what a viewer knows by that title.
- Everything else in your brief (DRAFT2.md or CHECK2.md) stands: ids, title strings, steps, groups, ties, writing rules. Wherever
  the brief says a name must be said or shown on screen, read it as: for these film people, the credited name is enough.
- Sacred Timeline people (uni "Sacred Timeline") use the Sacred Timeline id style of their example files ("st-firstname-lastname"
  for people added by the earlier batches, e.g. st-ruben-mackenzie); the other universes keep their prefixes (fox-, sv-, pr-).
